# Ballot Proposals 2 and 3
Certain land-use projects in New York City are currently subject to the Uniform Land Use Review Procedure[^1], a seven-month public review process, in which individual City Council members are given veto power[^5].
This includes any housing projects that involve:
	1. Zoning map amendments
	2. Special permits or zoning text amendments
	3. Disposition of city property
	4. Urban renewal plans or large-scale developments.
	5. Site selection for public families. [^2]
**Crucially,** most construction of new affordable housing requires changing the city's existing zoning maps, and is thus subject to ULURP.[^4]

Ballot Proposal #2 suggests exempting these projects from the ULURP process, and instead sending them directly through one of two processes:
1) 60-day review by Community Board followed by 30-day review by the NYC Board of Standards and Appeals.
or, in the 12 community districts with the lowest rates of affordable housing
2) Simultaneous review from Community Boards and the local Borough President, followed by a 45-day review from the City Planning Commission (appointed by the mayor).
